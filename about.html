The Firm

Built to give considered counsel, not just correct answers.

Zane & Zora is a full-service law firm serving corporations, institutions, and individuals across Nigeria and beyond.

Our Story

Founded on a simple premise

Zane & Zora was founded by lawyers who believed clients deserved more than technically correct advice — they deserved counsel that accounted for commercial reality, timing, and risk.

What began as a small commercial practice has grown into a full-service firm, while retaining the direct partner involvement and attention to detail that shaped its earliest matters.

Today, our lawyers advise across corporate transactions, disputes, finance, property, and intellectual property — always with the same standard: advice a client can act on with confidence.
