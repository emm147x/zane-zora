Practice Areas

Comprehensive legal expertise, precisely applied

Our lawyers work in focused practice groups, each with the depth of experience a serious matter requires.

Our Focus

Legal support built around the realities of business

Our practice groups are intentionally lean, partner-led, and built to provide clarity where speed, risk and precision matter most.

01 — Corporate & Commercial

Structuring for growth and governance

We advise founders, boards, and investors on company formation, mergers and acquisitions, joint ventures, private equity transactions, and day-to-day governance matters. Our role is to make sure the structure a business builds today doesn't create the problem it faces tomorrow.

02 — Litigation & Dispute Resolution

Representation before courts and tribunals

From commercial litigation to arbitration and alternative dispute resolution, we represent clients with a clear-eyed view of the strongest path to resolution — whether that means preparing for trial or negotiating a favourable settlement.

03 — Banking & Finance

Financing structures that hold up under scrutiny

We advise lenders, borrowers, and investors on loan facilities, security structures, regulatory compliance, and capital markets transactions — bringing clarity to some of the most heavily documented matters a business will encounter.

04 — Real Estate & Property

From acquisition through to title

We handle property acquisition, development agreements, leasing, and title perfection for commercial and residential clients — protecting the value of what is often a client's most significant asset.

05 — Intellectual Property

Protecting what makes a business distinct

We register, defend, and commercialise trademarks, patents, copyright, and trade secrets — helping clients turn intangible assets into a durable competitive advantage.

06 — Family & Estate Planning

Discretion, for matters that are personal

Wills, trusts, succession planning, and matrimonial matters are handled with the same rigor as our largest commercial mandates — and with the discretion these matters deserve.
